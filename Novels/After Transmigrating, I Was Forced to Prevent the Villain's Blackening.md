> [!synopsis] Synopsis
> Lu Jingyun is an obscure web novelist whose newest story suddenly becomes popular for one particular reason: its antagonist, Chu Cimu, is an irresistibly tragic beauty whose life seems specifically designed to make him suffer.
>
> Then Lu Jingyun is struck by lightning and wakes up inside his own novel.
>
> A deeply unreliable system gives him a single task: prevent Chu Cimu from blackening.
>
> Fortunately, every attempt begins early enough that Lu Jingyun can reach him before the worst events of his life take place. Unfortunately, the mission has already repeated more times than Lu Jingyun would like to count—and Chu Cimu is no ordinary fictional character.
>
> He remembers.
>
> After enduring abuse and torment within the story, Chu Cimu developed an awareness independent of the role written for him. Now he returns carrying memories of what came afterward, deeply suspicious of both the world around him and the relentlessly cheerful stranger who appears determined to protect him.
>
> Lu Jingyun nevertheless stays beside him, jokes with him, cares for him and repeatedly interferes whenever fate tries to push Chu Cimu towards the monster he is supposed to become.
>
> Eventually, Lu Jingyun sacrifices himself to save him and believes his task is finally complete.
>
> But when he opens his eyes again, decades have passed. The world no longer resembles the novel he wrote, his system has vanished, familiar characters have followed paths that should never have existed—and the villain he spent so long saving has blackened once again.
>
> Worse still, Chu Cimu has spent those years refusing to let him remain dead.
>
> What begins as a mission to rewrite a villain's fate gradually becomes a story about cycles, free will, obsession and two people discovering that neither of them can simply walk away from the world—or from each other.
