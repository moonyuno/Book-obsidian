> [!synopsis] Synopsis
> Xi Jianglan was once the most notorious young master in the Thirteen Provinces: wealthy, arrogant, extravagant, and completely protected by his powerful family. He made enemies wherever he went, but no one could touch him while the Xi family stood behind him. [112][113]
> 
> That protection disappears overnight when the entire Xi family is massacred. Xi Jianglan survives, but loses all of his cultivation and is reduced to a fragile, sickly figure who coughs blood after only a few steps. [112][113]
> 
> With his identity exposed and former enemies eager to settle old scores, Xi Jianglan has no choice but to hide his true background. He survives through shameless lies, feigned madness, and an impressive talent for acting helpless whenever it keeps a blade away from his throat. [112][113]
> 
> His carefully constructed act is put to the test when Sheng Jiao, one of the most powerful cultivators in the Thirteen Provinces—and someone Xi Jianglan cannot remember offending—finally corners him. [112]
> 
> Desperate, Xi Jianglan blurts out the first lie that might save his life: Sheng Jiao is secretly in love with him, and harming him would have terrible consequences. Instead of immediately killing him, Sheng Jiao pauses—and quietly asks whether that is true. [112][113]
> 
> What follows is a dangerous journey of revenge, hidden identities, political intrigue, and reluctant trust, as a sharp-tongued former young master tries to survive beside the cold, untouchable cultivator he once claimed to be in love with.
