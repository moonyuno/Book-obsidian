> [!synopsis] Synopsis
> Wang Yi has a very clear idea of what makes a proper villain.
>
> A villain should be ruthless, cruel and terrifying. He should torment the protagonist without mercy, make everyone's life miserable and, once his role in the story is complete, make a suitably dramatic exit.
>
> Unfortunately, Wang Yi is much better at understanding villainy in theory than actually being evil.
>
> After making a deal with a system, Wang Yi is sent through a succession of fictional worlds where his job is to occupy villainous roles and keep each story moving along its intended path. No matter what he personally thinks of the people around him, he has to follow the script: bully the protagonist, create conflict and eventually suffer whatever miserable fate has been prepared for the villain.
>
> His first target is Qin Xian.
>
> Outwardly, Wang Yi plays his part perfectly. He sneers, insults him and does everything possible to look like the vicious antagonist the story requires.
>
> Internally, things are rather different.
>
> Because Wang Yi actually thinks Qin Xian is ridiculously adorable.
>
> Normally this would not matter.
>
> Unfortunately, Qin Xian can read his mind.
>
> So while Wang Yi is standing over him trying desperately to look threatening, Qin Xian is simultaneously being subjected to an endless stream of affectionate thoughts, panicked commentary and embarrassingly cute mental reactions that completely contradict everything Wang Yi says aloud.
>
> And this problem does not disappear when the first story ends.
>
> Wang Yi continues moving through different worlds and identities, repeatedly cast as the villain opposite new versions of the protagonist—while somehow encountering the same soul again and again.
>
> Across different settings, lives and roles, Qin Xian continues being drawn toward the one man who is supposedly destined to hurt him.
>
> What begins as a ridiculous battle between Wang Yi's villainous script and his painfully obvious inner softness gradually becomes something much larger: a story about recurring lives, systems, identity and two people who keep finding each other regardless of what role the world assigns them.
