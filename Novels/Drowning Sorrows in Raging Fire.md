> [!synopsis] Synopsis
> In the modern world, most people are ordinary. Others are born with strange abilities: manipulating fire, seeing through walls, controlling time, or possessing powers that should belong to myth rather than reality.
>
> When those abilities cause trouble, the Anomaly Regulation Bureau steps in.
>
> Xuan Ji would prefer a relatively painless job, a decent salary, and perhaps enough money to deal with his increasingly alarming debt. Instead, his new position throws him directly into a supernatural disaster involving forbidden sacrifices, ancient powers and a man who should have been dead for three thousand years.
>
> Sheng Lingyuan was once Emperor Wu, the legendary ruler who ended an age of war between humans and supernatural races. History remembers him as both saviour and tyrant: brilliant, ruthless and terrifying enough to reshape the world itself.
>
> Three thousand years ago, he threw himself into the fiery depths of Chiyuan.
>
> Now someone has brought him back.
>
> Unfortunately for Xuan Ji, Sheng Lingyuan wakes up powerful, suspicious and entirely unimpressed by the modern world—or by the people attempting to contain him. Their first encounters involve rather more attempted murder than anyone at Xuan Ji's new workplace would probably consider ideal.
>
> Yet something between them feels strangely familiar.
>
> As supernatural incidents begin appearing across the country, Xuan Ji and Sheng Lingyuan are pulled into a conspiracy connected to the ancient seal that once separated humanity from the source of spiritual power. Solving it means uncovering what truly happened three thousand years ago, why Sheng Lingyuan chose to die, and why Xuan Ji seems inexplicably entangled with a history he cannot remember.
>
> Beneath the mysteries, political conspiracies and supernatural disasters lies a relationship shaped by loss, forgotten identities and thousands of years of unresolved grief—between an easy-going fire wielder who is far less ordinary than he appears and an ancient emperor who has never quite learned how to want to stay alive.
