- crown prince of [[Xian Le Nation]]

__Life as Crown Prince
- his parents were very proud of him
- He had no interest in the throne
- He wanted to save common folks (which he quoted pretty often)
- he saved a child from falling, revealing his face in the progress, which made all people cheer for him
- In the age of seventeen, he ascended to godhood, after the god of martial arts [[Jun Wu]] had seen him defeating a ghost
- The people in his country prayed for him and built statues, which lead to a peak in his power
- a war broke out in [[Xian Le Nation]]
- Xie Lian descended back into the human world, when he told Jun Wu, that he wanted to save common people 
- since he wasn’t very helpful in ending the war, the common people realized, that he was not the almighty goddess they had imagined
- They burned all his shrines
- He was now completely banished from godhood and lost his cultivation power
- To survive, he robbed, cursed and became desperate
- Many years later, he managed to ascend a second time
- As soon as he arrived, he murdered everyone in sight and descended again
- When the kingdom was destroyed, the people forgot about him
- Then, he somehow ascended again
