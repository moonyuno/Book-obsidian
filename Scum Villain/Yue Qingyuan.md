- Sect Leader of [[Cang Qiong Mountain Sect]]
- Peak Lord of Qiong Ding Peak
- originally named Yue Qi
- childhood friend of [[Shen Jiu]]
- sworn / adoptive brother figure to [[Shen Jiu]]
- one of the strongest cultivators of his era
- owner of Xuan Su sword

## Appearance

- handsome
- elegant
- composed
- gentle expression
- upright cultivator bearing
- dignified presence

## Personality

- kind
- patient
- protective
- self-sacrificing
- loyal
- deeply guilty
- restrained
- quietly stubborn
- indulgent toward [[Shen Jiu]]

## History

- orphan beggar in childhood
- lived on the streets with [[Shen Jiu]]
- promised to come back for him
- entered [[Cang Qiong Mountain Sect]]
- cultivated in order to save [[Shen Jiu]]
- suffered qi deviation in Ling Xi Caves
- soul bonded to Xuan Su sword
- unable to return in time
- carried lifelong guilt over failing [[Shen Jiu]]
- later became Sect Leader of [[Cang Qiong Mountain Sect]]
- first person to meet transmigrated [[Shen Yuan]]
- died in PIDW timeline while trying to save [[Shen Qingqiu]]

## Notes

- Xuan Su Sword

- one of the most tragic characters in SVSSS
- central to [[Shen Jiu]]'s backstory
- occasional user of the name "Xiao Jiu"
