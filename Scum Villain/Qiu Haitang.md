- daughter of House Qiu
- younger sister of [[Qiu Jianluo]]
- former fiancée of [[Shen Jiu]]
- took a liking to [[Shen Jiu]] when he was enslaved in her household
- one of [[Luo Binghe]]'s wives in PIDW
- major witness against [[Shen Qingqiu]] in PIDW
- key part of [[Shen Jiu]]'s downfall narrative

## Appearance

- young woman in the spring of her life
- coquettish voice
- face fair as magnolia
- gorgeously painted makeup
- slim figure
- ample bosom
- extraordinary beauty

## Personality

- emotional
- passionate
- impulsive
- vengeful
- deeply affected by betrayal
- capable of fierce hatred

## History

- daughter of House Qiu
- liked [[Shen Jiu]] as child
- brother allowed her to take him as fiancé
- discovered [[Shen Jiu]] during House Qiu massacre
- fainted on the spot
- woke and swore vengeance on [[Shen Jiu]]
- later testified against [[Shen Qingqiu]] in PIDW
- testimony ruined [[Shen Qingqiu]]'s reputation
- became one of [[Luo Binghe]]'s wives in PIDW
- fighting ability weak in original PIDW
- notably stronger in alternate timeline

## Notes

- one of the women most tied to [[Shen Jiu]]'s past
- tragic mix of victim and weaponized witness
- full name means begonia
