- large righteous cultivation sect
- considered one of the major powers of the righteous path
- famous for sword cultivation and strict rules
- birthplace / home sect of [[Su Xiyan]]
- deeply involved in [[Luo Binghe]]’s backstory
- often clashes with demon realm forces

## Structure

- led by the Old Palace Master
- Little Palace Mistress raised as successor
- many elite inner disciples
- focuses on sword techniques and large-scale formations
- strict hierarchy and emphasis on appearances
- maintains prestige and reputation above all

## History

- [[Su Xiyan]] served as head disciple here
- Old Palace Master became obsessively attached to [[Su Xiyan]]
- sect participated in the hunt and sealing of [[Tianlang-jun]]
- expelled [[Su Xiyan]] when she was suspected of demon ties
- involvement in cover-up around [[Luo Binghe]]’s birth
- later confronted over its role in these events

## Notes

- presents itself as righteous but hides deep hypocrisy
- major example of how “righteous sects” can be abusive
- key location for flashbacks in [[Luo Binghe]] and [[Su Xiyan]]’s story
