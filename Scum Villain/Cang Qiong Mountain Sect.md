- most powerful sect in the canon world of *Proud Immortal Demon Way*
- one of the main cultivation sects in SVSSS
- home sect of [[Shen Qingqiu]]
- home sect of [[Luo Binghe]]
- led by [[Yue Qingyuan]]
- centered on Cang Qiong Mountain
- divided into twelve peaks
- each peak ruled by its own Peak Lord
- most of the story connected to this sect

## Structure

- leadership peak overseeing the whole sect
- scholarly peak focused on literature, refinement, and the Four Arts
- logistics peak handling supplies, finances, rooms, repairs, and daily sect management
- medicine peak focused on healing, herbs, and treatment
- combat peak centered on martial training and fighting strength
- women-only peak for female disciples
- sword-focused peak
- overall twelve-peak hierarchy under the Sect Leader

## History

- long-established cultivation sect
- one of the dominant powers on the mainland
- [[Luo Binghe]]'s cultivation journey begins here
- repeatedly involved in conflicts with demons and rival sects
- central setting for many major SVSSS events

### Peaks
#### Qiong Ding Peak — [[Yue Qingyuan]]  
  - leadership peak  
  - sect master’s residence  
  - handles overall sect decisions and big gatherings  

#### Qing Jing Peak — [[Shen Qingqiu]]  
  - scholarly / etiquette peak  
  - focuses on cultivation, literature, and refinement  
  - known for quiet, rule-abiding disciples  

#### Bai Zhan Peak — [[Liu Qingge]]  
  - combat peak  
  - specializes in fighting, sparring, and real combat experience  
  - produces the strongest frontline fighters  

#### An Ding Peak — [[Shang Qinghua]]  
  - logistics and outer-sect peak  
  - handles money, supplies, information, and arrangements for disciples  
  - takes care of errands, housing, and day-to-day sect operations  

#### Qian Cao Peak — [[Mu Qingfang]]  
  - medicine and healing peak  
  - responsible for medical treatment, herbs, antidotes, and patients  
  - supports the sect in plagues and large-scale injuries  

#### Xian Shu Peak — [[Qi Qingqi]]  
  - women-only peak  
  - accepts only female disciples  
  - focuses on elegant sword cultivation and etiquette  

#### Wan Jian Peak — (sword-focused peak; peak lord not strongly emphasized in canon)  
- sword cultivation and sword arrays  
  - focuses on sword intent and large-scale sword techniques  

- Remaining peaks (less detailed or rarely mentioned)  
  - various focuses like formations, beast-taming, wandering cultivators, etc.  
  - each has its own Peak Lord but they are barely shown on-page

## Notes

- located in the east of the mainland
- Shuang Hu City lies within its territory
- one of the core settings of SVSSS
